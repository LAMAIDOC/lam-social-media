Frequently asked questions

How much do I earn?

30% of every payment a subscriber you referred makes, renewals included. Referred families get 20% off their first payment. For example: on an annual plan at $149.99/year, you earn $36.00 upfront, then $45.00 every year they renew. On a monthly plan at $24.99/month, you earn $6.00 the first month, then $7.50 every month they stay.

How long do I keep earning?

For as long as your referral stays subscribed. Every renewal pays you again, subject to the Ambassador Program Terms.

What does my community get?

20% off their first payment on either plan, plus a 7 day free trial on the annual plan. If they cancel before day 7, they aren’t charged.

How are referrals tracked?

Through your unique link, offer code or QR code. A new subscriber counts as yours when they redeem your offer code, or subscribe within 30 days of clicking your link or scanning your QR code. If they use an offer code, it takes priority. Families already in a LAM trial or subscription don’t count.

When and how do I get paid?

On the 15th of each month, for the payments made from the 16th of one month to the 15th of the next (a payment on October 10 is paid November 15; one on October 20 is paid December 15), through our ambassador platform, by bank transfer to the account you connect in your dashboard, or by PayPal if you prefer (just ask us). US bank payouts have no transfer fee. Outside the US, choose your local currency to avoid an international transfer fee. Payouts start once your balance reaches $50; smaller balances roll over to the next payout date, as long as your payout details stay up to date.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining is free. Share LAM with your community and earn from your referrals.

How should I talk about LAM?

LAM helps parents decide what to do next: watch at home, see a doctor, go to urgent care or the ER. It doesn’t diagnose and doesn’t replace a pediatrician. Every post needs a short disclaimer in the caption, such as “Not medical advice. Check with your doctor.” And always make it clear the post is an ad: in videos, say or show “ad” at the start and add the paid partnership label or #ad in the caption; in photo and carousel posts, start the caption with #ad; in Stories, show “Ad” or use the label; in newsletters and blogs, put “Ad” or “Affiliate link” right next to the LAM mention or link, with “Not medical advice. Check with your doctor.” near it.

Is LAM available on Android?

Not yet. LAM is on iPhone only for now, through the App Store. Let your audience know it’s iPhone-only so Android users aren’t disappointed.

Who can I contact for support?

Our ambassador team at ambassadors@littleangelmedical.com. We’re here to help you succeed.
