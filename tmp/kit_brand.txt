Keep it simple
• Use the logo as it is: don't stretch, recolor, rotate or add effects. Leave some empty space around it.
• Call the app LAM (said like "lamb") and tag @littleangelmedical.
• Fonts are optional. If you want to match us: Bebas Neue for headlines, Inter for text (both free on Google Fonts).
• Questions or need another format? ambassadors@littleangelmedical.com
