How these numbers are counted
  • Downloads, trials and paid by source come from AppsFlyer, by the day the event happened (UTC).
  • AppsFlyer also receives Apple test (sandbox) purchases, and they land under Organic. To remove them, totals for trials, paid and revenue come from Mixpanel live (production) events, and Organic is the total minus the paid sources.
  • Because the two tools can date the same purchase a day apart, Organic on a single day can look off. It evens out over a week or more.
  • Paid means a first payment: a trial that converted or a direct purchase. Renewals aren’t counted. Promo (free) accounts are left out.
  • Trial → paid compares people who paid in the period with people who started a trial in it. Trials take 7 days to convert, so recent trials haven’t had the chance yet.
  • “Organic / untracked” means the download came without a tracked ad or link: App Store search, our own social posts, influencers without a tracked link, word of mouth, and some team installs. It did not rise when Meta spend rose, so few Meta ad downloads are hiding in it.
  • All money is in USD. Meta spend is converted from Meta’s account currency at one fixed rate per month: the Bank of Canada average of the month before (October 2026: 1.3962, November 2026: 1.4135). Revenue is Apple’s gross, before Apple’s cut.
